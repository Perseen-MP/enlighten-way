Data & Decision Intelligence

AI & Data Specialist

₹5L - ₹16L / yr

Extracts actionable business insights from raw datasets, builds automated dashboards, and integrates machine learning models.

Core Stack: Python, SQL, Pandas, Power BI / Tableau, Scikit-Learn

Reality Check (The Bright Side): Massive demand across fintech, healthcare, and retail; high starting compensation.
